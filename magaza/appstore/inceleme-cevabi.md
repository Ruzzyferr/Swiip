# App Review 2.1 — cevap taslağı

**Durum:** 1.0 sürümü 2026-08-22'de reddedildi. Gerekçe **Guideline 2.1 — Information
Needed**, yani "uygulamada hata bulduk" değil, "yeni uygulamayı inceleyebilmek için
şu sekiz bilgiyi istiyoruz". İlk gönderimlerin çoğu bunu alır.

Cevap Resolution Center'dan yazılıyor:
`App Store Connect → Swiip → Distribution → App Review → iOS Submission → Reply to App
Review`. Aynı metin ileride `App Review Information → Notes` alanına da konmalı; Apple
bunu açıkça istiyor.

## Bende olmayan iki şey

**1. Ekran kaydı.** Apple **fiziksel cihazda**, güncel işletim sistemiyle çekilmiş bir
kayıt istiyor — simülatör kabul edilmiyor. Uygulamanın açılışından başlayıp şu akışları
sırayla göstermeli:

1. Uygulamayı açış (soğuk başlangıç)
2. Kayıt olma, çıkış, tekrar giriş (kayıtta e-posta doğrulama adımı yok — kod yalnızca
   parola sıfırlamada gönderiliyor; istersen onu da göster)
3. Değerlendirmenin ilk bloğu (birkaç soru yeter, 134'ünün hepsi değil)
4. Kamera izni istemi ve vücut fotoğrafı çekimi; ardından fotoğrafın silindiğini
   söyleyen ekran
5. Program sekmesi: bir hareketin detayı ve "neden bu hareket" gerekçesi
6. Beslenme sekmesi: öğün planı, kaydırmalı öğün değiştirme, barkod okuma
7. Kilitli bir özelliğe dokunup paywall'a düşme → plan seçme → **satın alma sayfasının
   açılması** (sandbox hesabıyla)
8. Ayarlar: bildirim izni istemi, "Aboneliği iptal et", "Hesabımı sil" akışı sonuna kadar

**2. Test edilen cihaz ve işletim sistemi listesi.** Aşağıdaki 2. maddeyi hangi
iPhone modellerinde ve hangi iOS sürümünde denediğinle doldur. Uydurmadım.

> **3. What the app does and who it is for**
> Swiip is a personal training and nutrition coaching app. The user answers a 134-question
> assessment covering training history, available equipment, weekly schedule, injuries and
> medical flags, food preferences and dietary constraints, and may optionally submit one
> body photo. From those answers a deterministic engine produces a weekly training program
> and a daily meal plan.
>
> The feature that distinguishes the app is that every decision is inspectable. For each
> exercise and each meal the app shows which of the user's own answers and which rules
> produced it — for example, "a hip thrust was selected instead of a floor deadlift because
> you reported a lumbar disc herniation". This reasoning is a stored decision trace from the
> solver, not text generated by a language model.
>
> Target audience: adults aged 18 and over who train in a gym or at home and want a
> structured program plus nutrition guidance, primarily in Turkish. The problem it solves:
> comparable apps hand out a static program without explaining it, so the user cannot tell
> whether it accounts for their injuries, their equipment or their schedule.
>
> **4. How to set up and access the main features**
> A demo account is provided in App Review Information:
> `inceleme@swiip.app` / `mercan-defter-7431-yelken`
>
> This account's assessment is already completed and a program has been generated, so every
> screen is reachable immediately after signing in. This matters because the app opens on
> the assessment and the program, nutrition and coach tabs stay locked until it is finished
> — a program cannot be computed without answers. To see the flow from zero instead, please
> register a new account and complete the assessment; it takes roughly twelve minutes.
> No sample files are required.
>
> **5. External services used to deliver core functionality**
> - Vercel AI Gateway, routing to Anthropic Claude models (Opus 5, Sonnet 5, Haiku 4.5).
>   AI is used at exactly four points: interpreting the assessment answers, analysing the
>   body photo, identifying which dish is in a food photo, and the coach chat. Every numeric
>   value — training volume, progression, calories, macronutrients, nutrient content — is
>   computed by deterministic formulas and read from our own database, never from a model.
> - RevenueCat — subscription entitlement management and receipt validation.
> - Resend (EU region) — transactional email only, for password reset codes.
> - Open Food Facts — barcode lookup for packaged foods.
> - Our own server and PostgreSQL database — the API and all application data.
> - Exercise photographs come from free-exercise-db, which is public domain, and are bundled
>   in the app binary rather than fetched at runtime.
>
> The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind, which
> is why it does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt.
>
> The body photo is uploaded over TLS, analysed, and deleted immediately afterwards. It is
> never written to disk on the server; only the numeric measurements derived from it are
> stored.
>
> **6. Regional differences**
> There are none. The features and the content are identical in every region. The app ships
> in Turkish and English, with Turkish as the default. The language follows the account
> setting rather than the device language, deliberately: many users in Turkey run their phone
> in English but want the app in Turkish. One behaviour looks regional but is not: if the user answers that they fast, the
> meal slots are renamed to sahur and iftar during Ramadan. That is driven by the user's own
> answer, not by their storefront, and it changes labels rather than features.
>
> **7. Regulated industry and third-party material**
> Swiip provides general fitness and nutrition information. It does not diagnose or treat any
> medical condition, makes no medical claims, and contains no medical-device functionality.
> Diagnostic language is prohibited in the product copy.
>
> Four hard safety gates stop program generation and direct the user to a physician: age
> under 18, pregnancy, cardiac warning signs, and a positive eating-disorder screen. These
> gates cannot be skipped or dismissed. Estimates such as body-fat percentage are always
> presented as a range, never as a single number.
>
> No licensed or protected third-party material is used. Exercise photographs are public
> domain (free-exercise-db). Food composition values are our own curated dataset.
>
> **8. In-App Purchase summary**
> Two auto-renewable subscription tiers, each offered monthly and annually, in the
> "Swiip Üyelik" subscription group:
>
> - Swiip Temel — ₺99 per month or ₺690 per year
> - Swiip Pro — ₺169 per month or ₺1,190 per year
>
> The free tier includes the full assessment, one body analysis, day one of the training
> program, and manual calorie entry.
> Temel adds every day of the weekly program, post-session feedback and adaptation, calorie
> and macronutrient targets, meal planning with recipes and a pantry, barcode scanning, and
> 60 coach messages per month.
> Pro adds food recognition from a photo (250 per month) and raises the coach quota to 150
> messages per month.
>
> How to reach the purchase screen: Settings tab → "Planlar", or tap any locked feature
> (for example the second training day, the meal plan, or food recognition). Both routes
> open the same screen. It shows the title, the billing period and the price of each
> subscription, and once a plan is selected it shows the exact amount to be charged and the
> renewal date in the largest type on the screen. No plan is preselected. There is no
> countdown and no artificial scarcity. Terms of use and the privacy policy are linked at
> the bottom of that screen. Cancelling takes two taps from the top of the Settings tab.
>
> Account deletion is available in the app, in the Settings tab, and permanently deletes the
> account and its data.
