On weekdays we usually reply the same day, and within two business days at the latest. You will get a faster answer if you include: your phone model, the app version (it is at the bottom of Settings) and which screen the problem is on.

Frequently asked

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Plan and quota → Cancel. One tap, no hidden step. After cancelling, every feature stays open until the end of the period. You can also cancel from your App Store or Google Play account subscription settings.

I paid but Pro did not unlock

First close and reopen the app. If it still does not unlock, tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the plan screen. If that does not work, write to us: we can see the store receipt and unlock it by hand.

I do not like the program — can I change it?

Yes, without limits. The swap button next to each exercise shows alternatives from the same muscle group. Editing your program is free on every plan.

I want my data deleted

The account deletion page explains how. You can export your data before deleting it.

Where do the numbers come from?

We list the exact equations behind every calorie, macro and load calculation on the calculation sources page. The same list is in the app under Settings → Calculation sources.

A note about health

Swiip is not a medical device and does not diagnose. The numbers it gives are estimates derived from measurements and images. If you have a health concern, talk to your doctor; in an emergency, call your local emergency number.
