What it does

Eight cards, about four minutes

The assessment asks what actually changes your program — where you train, what equipment you can reach, how many days you really have, what you will not eat. Nothing is asked twice, and nothing is asked that changes nothing.

Every decision is traceable

Under each exercise you can read why it is there: which answer, which rule. The explanation is not a sentence an AI made up — it is the solver's decision trail.

The maths is a formula, the language is AI

Volume, progressive overload, deload, 1RM, calories and macros are all deterministic formulas. Language models are inconsistent at arithmetic, and in a health context that is not acceptable. Add the same food twice and you get the same macros — every time.

Calorie tracking that stays free

Manual food logging and search are unlimited on every plan, including the free one. Nutrition values always come from a database, never from a language model.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No badges, streaks, confetti or celebration animations.
  • No ads or upsells for people who already pay. Not one line.
  • Cancelling is one tap, at the top of Settings, never hidden.
  • Your body photo is deleted the moment it is analysed. Only the measurements are kept.
  • Estimates are shown as ranges, never as a single number.

Where the numbers come from

Every calorie, macro and load calculation comes from a published equation, and we list them one by one on the calculation sources page — with the journal and the DOI.
